# Player detection: a general person detector replaces the football weights for people (2026-11-22)

## What was wrong

On the labelled SoccerNet clips the pipeline found 47% of players at 37% precision. Binning every labelled player and every box of ours (`workers/video/detection_diagnosis.py`) showed why: on closer views the football weights box pieces of people. Over 1,500 sampled frames of ten clips they produced about 11,700 piece boxes at confidence 0.45, and found only 22% of players between 120 and 240 px tall. At 1920 px input they found 5%.

## What was compared

`deploy/modal_detector_eval.py` ran each detector once on every fifth frame of the 13 stored clips and saved the raw boxes; `scripts/score_detectors.py` scores any threshold or filter from that file on a CPU. Ten clips were used to choose; three (SNGS-118, 138, 193) were held out.

Detection only, players and goalkeepers, confidence 0.4, field filter and suppression:

| Detector for people | Dev recall | Dev precision | Held-out recall | Held-out precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stored pipeline (football weights) | 46% | 37% | 51% | 39% |
| COCO YOLOv8m at 1280 | 89% | 82% | 87% | 83% |
| COCO YOLO11x at 1280 | 92% | 83% | 89% | 84% |

YOLO11x at 1920 was no better than at 1280. Joining stacked piece boxes from the football weights helped by about one point and is not used.

## One role per track

A general detector cannot tell a referee from a player, and the football weights name a referee in only some frames of a track: on the ten clips, true referee tracks were called a referee in 17% of their frames, players in 0%. So each track now gets one role from all its frames (`people_boxes.settle_roles`): a referee when at least 15% of its frames (and at least five) were called one, or at least 5% were and the track is in neither kit group half the time; a goalkeeper when at least 10% were called one. The frame's own call is kept as `role_hint`.

Whole pipeline with YOLOv8m for people, before and after this rule:

| | Without | With |
|---|---|---|
| Development clips: recall / precision | 90.1% / 82.7% | 90.2% / 84.2% |
| Held-out clips: recall / precision | 89.3% / 85.7% | 89.5% / 88.5% |
| Labelled players followed (development, of 214) | 210 | 209 |

The rule was chosen on the ten development clips, where it marks 36% of referee observations and 0.15% of outfield players' observations as referees; on the three held-out clips, 49% and none.

## People beside the pitch

A general person detector also finds coaches, substitutes, stewards and ball boys, and they were tracked as players: they took kit groups, touches and distance. Where a clip has a pitch calibration, each track's foot point is now placed on the pitch map, and a track that lies more than 1 m outside the outline in at least 80% of its placed frames (10 frames or more) is set aside as `off_pitch` (`workers/video/off_pitch.py`). It is done before tracks are linked, so these people never become player identities. A settled referee is never set aside, and a player who steps out for a throw-in is inside for most of the track and is kept.

Worked out from the stored tracks of the labelled runs (YOLOv8m people, kit fix), not from a fresh run:

| | Tracks set aside | Player observations lost | Boxes on nobody labelled that go | Precision of player boxes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Development clips (10) | 29 of 570 | 95 of 88,678 (0.11%, one track) | 2,274 of 11,568 (20%) | 85.5% to 87.5% |
| Held-out clips (3) | 8 of 158 | 0 of 27,347 | 349 of 2,160 (16%) | 89.2% to 90.2% |

Margins from 0 to 5 m and shares from 60% to 100% were compared on the development clips; wider margins removed fewer bystanders and saved no players. All thirteen labelled clips fail the line check, so this is measured where the calibration is poor. It has not been measured on labelled footage whose lines match, which is where it should work best. On the Bundesliga demo clip, whose lines match, it set aside 10 tracks; drawn on the frames, all were coaches and staff in the technical areas or stewards behind the boards, and no player; the assistant referee on that touchline kept the referee role. Player identities went from 32 to 23, none without a kit group, and the mean number of players in view from 24.0 to 20.5.

What stays: people beside the pitch in frames with no calibration, tracks shorter than 10 placed frames, and anyone standing on the pitch who is not a player.

## What it does not fix

- **Pitch positions.** Median error per clip is still 3.5 to 21 m; that is the calibration, not the boxes.
- **Distance** is still overstated, by about 1.35 times.
- **Split identities.** Each followed player is still about 1.8 identities.
- **Most referees.** The rule above marks roughly a third to a half of referee observations; the rest are still counted as players. Telling a referee by kit colour would need a third colour group, which is not built.
- **Small players.** Under 60 px tall, recall is 53% (318 labelled boxes), which matters for a fixed wide camera.
- **One-digit shirt numbers** got worse with more players read (20 wrong of 22), so they stay unproposed.

## What changed in the code

- `deploy/modal_clip_run.py` analyses one of your own clips on a GPU with the code in the checkout and stores the run on the evaluation volume, without using the deployed worker.
- `FootballDetector` takes optional `person_weights` (`YOLO_PERSON_MODEL_PATH`, or `models/person.pt`). People then come from that model; the football weights still give the ball and the goalkeeper/referee labels (`people_boxes.adopt_roles`). Unset, nothing changes.
- The hosted worker (`deploy/modal_app.py`) is configured to use YOLOv8m for people, the file it already ships as the secondary ball model, and its limit is raised from 600 s to 1200 s because a 120 s clip would otherwise run out of time. It has not been redeployed, and a full 120 s clip has not been timed.

Commands: `python -m modal run deploy/modal_detector_eval.py --out-dir <folder>`, then `python scripts/score_detectors.py <folder>`; and `python -m modal run deploy/modal_gsr_eval.py --out-dir <folder> --sequences <clips> --label yolov8m --person yolov8m`.
