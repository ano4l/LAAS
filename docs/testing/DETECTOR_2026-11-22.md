# Player detection: a general person detector replaces the football weights for people (2026-11-22)

## What was wrong

On the labelled SoccerNet clips the pipeline found 47% of players at 37% precision. Binning every labelled player and every box of ours (`workers/video/detection_diagnosis.py`) showed why: on closer views the football weights box pieces of people. Over 1,500 sampled frames of ten clips they produced about 11,700 piece boxes at confidence 0.45, and found only 22% of players between 120 and 240 px tall. At 1920 px input they found 5%.

## What was compared

`deploy/modal_detector_eval.py` ran each detector once on every fifth frame of the 13 stored clips and saved the raw boxes; `scripts/score_detectors.py` scores any threshold or filter from that file on a CPU. Ten clips were used to choose; three (SNGS-118, 138, 193) were held out.

Detection only, players and goalkeepers, confidence 0.4, field filter and suppression:

| Detector for people | Dev recall | Dev precision | Held-out recall | Held-out precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stored pipeline (football weights) | 46% | 37% | 51% | 39% |
| COCO YOLOv8m at 1280 | 89% | 82% | 87% | 83% |
| COCO YOLO11x at 1280 | 92% | 83% | 89% | 84% |

YOLO11x at 1920 was no better than at 1280. Joining stacked piece boxes from the football weights helped by about one point and is not used.

`deploy/modal_gsr_eval.py --person ...`, same clips, same scoring as the 15 November evaluation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Players found (recall) | 46.5% | 90.1% | 92.3% |

| Kit group agrees with labelled side | 306 of 315 | 359 of 376 | 349 of 367 |

Held-out clips: recall 51.9% to 89.3% (YOLOv8m) and 91.6% (YOLO11x); precision 40.2% to 85.7% and 86.2%; players followed 50 to 61 of 63; identities per followed player 2.48 to 1.80.

## What it does not fix

- **Pitch positions.** Median error per clip is still 3.5 to 21 m; that is the calibration, not the boxes.
- **Distance** is still overstated, by about 1.35 times.
- **Split identities.** Each followed player is still about 1.8 identities.
- **Referees.** A general detector cannot tell a referee from a player. About 6% of its boxes are referees counted as players; the football weights' own referee label recovers only some.
- **Small players.** Under 60 px tall, recall is 53% (318 labelled boxes), which matters for a fixed wide camera.
- **One-digit shirt numbers** got worse with more players read (20 wrong of 22), so they stay unproposed.

## What changed in the code

- `FootballDetector` takes optional `person_weights` (`YOLO_PERSON_MODEL_PATH`, or `models/person.pt`). People then come from that model; the football weights still give the ball and the goalkeeper/referee labels (`people_boxes.adopt_roles`). Unset, nothing changes.
- The hosted worker (`deploy/modal_app.py`) is not yet configured to use it. Its 600 s limit for a 120 s clip needs checking against the longer processing time first.

Commands: `python -m modal run deploy/modal_detector_eval.py --out-dir <folder>`, then `python scripts/score_detectors.py <folder>`; and `python -m modal run deploy/modal_gsr_eval.py --out-dir <folder> --sequences <clips> --label yolov8m --person yolov8m`.
