# Offline ball path and image-based camera motion, 14 November 2026

## What changed

- `workers/video/camera_motion.py`: per-frame camera motion taken from the tracker's own frame-to-frame affine estimate (image features), saved as `camera-motion.json` in each run. A fallback from player movement is used when the tracker provides none.
- `workers/video/ball_path.py`: after the clip is processed, one ball candidate per frame is chosen as the most consistent path through all candidates. `ball_selected` now holds this offline choice; the previous frame-by-frame choice is kept as `ball_selected_online`. Selected rows also carry `ball_path_segment` and `ball_path_support` (`confident` or `weak_between_confident`).
- Measurements and insights are computed after this step, so they use the offline choice.

Only observed detections are selected. Nothing is interpolated.

## Rules

1. A candidate continues a path if it lies within `2% + 50% x gap seconds` of the image diagonal of the previous one (stabilised coordinates, gap up to 1 second); otherwise it starts a new segment at a cost.
2. Weak candidates (confidence below 0.5) at a spot where detections recur for 1.5 seconds or more, with no detection there reaching 0.5, are removed first. These are sideline objects and pitch markings. This relies on image-based camera motion: a fixed sideline object in `dfl_test_1` stays within about 12 pixels over 220 frames once stabilised, while the player-movement estimate let it drift by over 200.
3. Every segment needs at least one detection of 0.35 or more.
4. A weak detection (below 0.35) is kept only when confident ones on the same segment lie within 0.6 seconds before and after it.

## Visual inspection (by the developer's AI assistant, not human ground truth)

Contact sheets of 40 sampled crops were inspected for each group.

- **Dropped picks** (39 sampled, before rule 4 was added): about 24 were clearly not the ball (the `dfl_test_1` sideline object, pitch-line blobs, boots, a person), about 6 looked like a real ball, the rest were unclear.
- **Added weak picks** (40 of 97 sampled, final rules): about 23 looked like the ball (mostly motion-blurred), about 6 were clearly not (boots, a line blob, a corner mark), about 11 were unclear.

Before rules 2 to 4, at least 12 of 40 sampled added picks were clearly wrong.

## Limitations

- No labelled ball positions exist for these clips, so precision and recall are not measured. The visual tallies above are approximate judgements of small crops.
- Boots next to a confidently detected ball can still be selected as weak picks.
- A resting ball that is never detected with confidence 0.5 or more is treated as a fixed object and removed.
- Track linking does not use image-based camera motion: on the cut-repair check it rejoined 497 cuts with 30 wrong joins, against 520 and 23 with the player-movement estimate, so the linker keeps the latter.
- Not run on GPU or Modal. `camera-motion.json` is not in the Modal artifact list.
