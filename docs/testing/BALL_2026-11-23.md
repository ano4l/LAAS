# Ball detection: the general detector's ball class (2026-11-23)

## What was wrong

On the 13 labelled SoccerNet clips the ball is labelled in 6,568 of 7,500 frames (10 development clips) and 2,094 of 2,250 (3 held-out clips). Scored against those labels (`workers/video/ball_benchmark.py`), the stored pipeline runs found the ball badly:

| | Development | Held-out |
|---|---|---|
| A ball candidate on the ball | 11.2% of frames | 8.9% |
| Chosen ball is the ball | 8.1% | 5.9% |
| Chosen balls that are the ball | 19.8% | 15.3% |

Two thirds of the misses were the ball never being detected at all, so the path selection could not help.

## What was compared

`deploy/modal_ball_eval.py` ran each detector on every fifth frame of the 13 clips at a low threshold; `scripts/score_ball_detectors.py` scored it on a CPU. Share of labelled-ball frames with a box on the ball, at confidence 0.1:

| Detector | Development | Held-out | ms per frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| Football weights, 1280 (in use) | 6.8% | 6.7% | 55 |
| Football weights, 1920 | 0.2% | 0.2% | 87 |
| Football weights, 960 px tiles | 0.7% | 0.7% | 244 |
| General YOLOv8m, 1280 | **60.8%** | **59.9%** | 52 |
| General YOLO11x, 1280 | 60.7% | 56.3% | 131 |
| General YOLOv8m, 960 px tiles | 65.5% | 59.9% | 282 |

The football weights miss the ball whatever its size (1% of balls 18 px and larger), so neither a bigger image nor tiling helps them. The general COCO detector's "sports ball" class finds it about nine times as often. Tiling adds a few points for five times the cost and many more false boxes, so it is not used.

## What changed

The general detector already runs on every frame to find people (`docs/testing/DETECTOR_2026-11-22.md`). Its ball class is now taken from that same pass (`workers/video/detector.py`) and added to the ball candidates, at no extra cost. The football weights' own ball boxes are still candidates too, and the path selection chooses between them as before.

Whole pipeline on the 13 clips (YOLOv8m, as the hosted worker is configured):

| | Development before | after | Held-out before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A ball candidate on the ball | 11.2% | **60.9%** | 8.9% | **58.8%** |
| Chosen ball is the ball | 8.1% | **46.3%** | 5.9% | **45.8%** |
| Chosen balls that are the ball | 19.8% | 52.5% | 15.3% | 51.5% |

People are unchanged: recall 90.0%, 1.79 identities per followed player.

On the Bundesliga demo clip (YOLO11x people), the chosen ball goes from 62% to 74% of frames. None of the 467 balls chosen before was lost, and a sample of 12 of the 90 newly chosen ones were all the real ball. Controlled touches go from 17 to 24, and possession supported by the model from 12.8% to 21.1% of playing time. The six passes are unchanged and still match the footage (`docs/testing/PASSES_2026-11-22.md`).

## Tried and dropped

**Rejecting long boxes.** Many wrong choices on the labelled clips are boots, yellow goalkeeper gloves and hi-vis jackets, which give long boxes, so boxes more than 1.5 times as long as wide were dropped. On the labelled clips that helped a little: chosen ball right 46.3% to 48.6% (development) and 45.8% to 47.8% (held-out). On the demo clip every one of the 57 frames it dropped was the real ball, blurred into a long box in flight at 18–30 m/s, and the clip lost two correctly found passes and gained a wrong one. The broadcast footage's motion blur makes shape unsafe, so it is not used.

## What it does not fix

- **Wrong balls.** In about a third of labelled frames the chosen ball is something else: a boot, a glove, a jacket, a shape on an advert board. Half of all chosen balls are right, not most.
- **The ball at a player's feet.** When the ball is against a player in the image it is chosen correctly 23% of the time, against 59% in the open. That is where touches happen.
- **Still missed.** The ball is not detected at all in 18% of labelled frames.
- **YOLO11x** put its most confident box on the ball more often (53% against 48% for YOLOv8m) with fewer false boxes, but is two and a half times slower. The hosted worker uses YOLOv8m.
- **Existing runs** keep their old ball candidates until the clip is processed again. The hosted worker has not been redeployed.

Commands: `python -m modal run deploy/modal_ball_eval.py --out-dir <folder>`, then `python scripts/score_ball_detectors.py <folder>`; the whole-pipeline ball score is part of `deploy/modal_gsr_eval.py`'s report (`ball` and `ball_path_again`).
