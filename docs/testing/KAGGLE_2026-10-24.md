## Interpretation

Functional checks passed for these five clips: completed real inference, persisted metrics/tracking, signed artifact download, matching source SHA256, full exported decode count and matching duration. Every downloaded tracking row has model_detection provenance; observations extend to each final source frame. The live frontend was checked with the new Bundesliga result and loaded its 30-second video at readyState 4. All five results remain in the production Library.

These runs do **not** pass acceptance for highly accurate football analytics. Sampled visual inspection shows duplicate boxes around the same player on input_vid and Untitled design, plus an incorrect player detection on the broadcast logo in Untitled design. The resulting player counts and identity fragmentation are not reliable player statistics. Even the Bundesliga runs show missed ball candidates and ambiguous multiple-ball frames.

Ball candidate coverage counts frames with at least one prediction. It is not accuracy or recall, and extra false positives can increase it. No manually reviewed ground-truth ball boxes or stable player identities were used for scoring; ball precision, recall, identity switches and event correctness remain unmeasured. Temporary track counts are not distinct football players. Image movement is not physical running distance.

Recommended priorities from this test: investigate duplicate player detections and class confusion, validate confidence/NMS thresholds on reviewed frames, add pitch-region exclusion, handle shot changes, and evaluate a specialist small-ball detector against independent reviewed clips. Keep passes, possession and calibrated workload analytics unavailable until their supporting data is validated.
