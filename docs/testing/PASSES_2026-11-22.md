# Passes and completion estimated from the ball's path (2026-11-22)

## What was wrong

The Analytics row for passes guessed from pairs of touches: two touches in a row by different players of one kit group, within 5 seconds, were taken as a pass. On the 30-second Bundesliga demo clip that gave about 3 passes from 17 touches, and completion was not estimated at all.

Looking at the ball's path on the pitch map for that clip showed a second problem. The detector takes the painted penalty spots for the ball. 308 of the 823 ball candidates in the clip sat on one of the two spots, 147 of the 545 chosen ball positions were a spot, and the chosen path kept jumping between the real ball and a spot 40 m away. The "ball seen" figure of 72.7% was therefore too high.

## What changed

**Painted spots are no longer the ball** (`workers/video/ball_path.py`, `ball-path-2`). With a pitch calibration, candidates that sit still on a penalty spot or the centre spot on the pitch map for 1.5 seconds or more, with nobody within 2 m for at least 70% of that time, are dropped before the path is chosen. On the demo clip all 308 are dropped, the path goes from 29 segments to 3, and the real ball is picked up in frames where a spot had been chosen instead. The honest ball figure for the clip is 62.3% of frames, not 72.7%. A ball on the centre spot with a player standing over it is kept. A ball left alone on a penalty spot is not seen until it is struck.

**Passes come from the ball's path** (`workers/video/pass_estimates.py`). A player has the ball while it is within 2.5 m of them on the pitch map and slower than 9 m/s, and it either moves with them, changes course at them, or matches a controlled touch already found in the image. A pass is counted when one player had the ball, the ball was then seen travelling at 6 m/s or more, and a different player at least 4 m away had it within 6 seconds. The next player being a team-mate makes it completed; being in the other kit group makes it not completed. A ball that is still beside the passer half a second later with an opponent nearer to it was taken, not passed, and is left to the tackle rule. A ball struck at 12 m/s or more that is not seen to reach anyone is listed apart and counted as neither.

It needs the pitch calibration, because the camera's own movement has to be taken out of the ball's speed. Without one, the row falls back to the touch-pair guess.

**In the app.** The Analytics row "Passes and completion" shows passes seen, how many reached a team-mate, a percentage and mean length per kit group, labelled as an estimate. The home and away row and the player rows use the same figures. Each pass is in the possible-event list under Ball; "Draft under Review" now fills in the outcome and the start and end places on the pitch as well as the time, for a person to check and approve. Nothing is counted as an event until then.

## Checked against the footage of the demo clip

I watched frames around each pass the rule found. This is my reading of one clip, not labelled data.

| Time | The rule says | The footage shows |
|---|---|---|
| 2.0 s | L11 to L19, completed, 15 m | White 11 passes to white 19. Right. |
| 4.7 s | L19 to L25, completed, 25 m | White 19 plays it back to the goalkeeper. Right. |
| 12.0 s | L25 to L23, not completed, 39 m | The goalkeeper's long ball is contested in midfield by a white and a green player. The tracker had swapped those two players' identities just before, so who won it cannot be trusted. Unclear. |
| 20.8 s | L23 to L29, not completed, 21 m | Green 23's ball into the area is collected by the other goalkeeper. Right. |
| 22.6 s | L29 to L7, not completed, 28 m | That goalkeeper's clearance goes to green 7. Right. |
| 24.6 s | L7 to L4, completed, 8 m | Green 7 lays it off to green 4. Right. |

One false pass was found and fixed while checking: green 4 was tackled by white 18 and the loose ball was first listed as a misplaced pass. That is what the "taken, not passed" rule is for.

Missed: between 15 and 19 seconds the green side wins the ball, plays it long, a team-mate flicks it on and green 23 runs onto it. The ball is in the air or not detected for most of that, and nothing is counted. So the clip has about seven or eight passes by eye and the rule finds six.

On the second local clip (`dfl-test-11-30s.mp4`) the rule finds four completed passes between two players of one side and two long balls. I did not check those against the footage.

## What it does not fix

- **It is not validated.** No labelled passes exist for this footage. The thresholds were set by reasoning and one clip. Tagging the passes on a clip by hand under Review would give a yardstick to score it against.
- **Missing ball.** The ball is still undetected in over a third of the demo clip's frames, and passes in those stretches are missing.
- **Balls in the air.** A flying ball lands in the wrong place on a flat pitch map, so headers and flick-ons are usually not seen as touches.
- **Swapped identities.** When two players cross and the tracker swaps them, a pass is credited to the wrong kit group. It happened once in the demo clip.
- **Not completed is broad.** It covers clearances, blocked crosses and loose balls as well as misplaced passes.
- **Existing runs.** A run processed before this change gets pass estimates when it is next opened, but its ball path is not recomputed, so painted spots stay in it until the clip is processed again. The hosted worker has not been redeployed.
